It's finally happening on 20 June – we can't wait to welcome you to the state capital Wiesbaden! To make sure your race day runs smoothly, we've put together all the important information. Please take a moment to read through the updates.

🛑 New Event Location & Updated Course

Wiesbaden will be buzzing on 20 June: a FIFA World Cup match will fill the city centre and pedestrian zones, and major construction work is ongoing at Schlossplatz. To ensure a safe and smooth race experience, we have relocated the event in coordination with the authorities:

  • → New event location & Start/Finish: Kranzplatz (not Schlossplatz!) – this is where you'll find the Expo, bib collection, Start, Finish, toilets and refreshments.
  • → Updated course: The route has been adjusted to avoid fan crowds in the pedestrian zone and the construction site. Current course maps are below.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Where exactly is the start and finish? +
In 2026, start and finish are at Kranzplatz, 65183 Wiesbaden – not at Schlossplatz. The change was made due to a FIFA World Cup match and construction works at Schlossplatz.
I haven't received my confirmation email – what should I do? +
Please check your spam/junk folder first. During race week (approx. 15/16 June) the confirmation email with QR code will be resent to all participants. If it's still missing after that: info@wiesbaden-marathon.de
My race bib hasn't arrived – what now? +
Dispatch starts from 8 June. Please only contact us if nothing has arrived by Tuesday, 17 June. After that, replacement bibs are available at the collection point (bring photo ID).
Can I still change my distance? +
Yes, by email to info@wiesbaden-marathon.de or in person at the bib collection. Longer distance: Pay the difference. Shorter distance: no refund.
Can I still withdraw? Is a refund possible? +
Since 01.06.2026 no refunds are possible. In case of illness or injury, a goodwill request with medical certificate may be submitted – by email to info@wiesbaden-marathon.de.
Can someone else collect my race bib? +
Yes, with an authorisation form and a copy of the registered participant's ID. Download here: Authorisation PDF →
Can a minor participate? +
Yes. Minor participants need a written parental consent. An informal letter with name, date of birth, distance and signature is sufficient. Template: Parental consent PDF →
Do I have an RMV public transport ticket? +
The RMV combo ticket is only valid for participants who booked it at registration. It is not a separate ticket – the RMV Combo logo is integrated in the lower section of your registration confirmation and serves as your ticket together with that document. Valid on race day for travel across the entire RMV network. Cannot be booked retrospectively.
Where is the best place to park? +
Parkhaus Theater (~10 Min Fußweg), Parkhaus Bowling Green (~5 Min) oder Parkhaus Luisenplatz (~5 Min, no restrictions 2026). Ab 18:15 Uhr ist die Laufstrecke gesperrt – bitte mindestens 90 Minuten vor der Startzeit anreisen. ÖPNV wird empfohlen: Bus 1/8 → Haltestelle Kochbrunnenplatz.
What happens if I lose my chip? +
The chip is integrated into the race bib – please do not bend it! In case of loss or damage, go to the bib collection point immediately.
What are the age categories? +
3 km: born 2015–2019 (ages 6–12). 5 km: from age 12 (born 2014). 10 km & Half Marathon: from age 16 (born 2010) – with parental consent.
Do all finishers receive a medal? +
Yes – all finishers of all distances receive the official finisher medal. Engraving (name + time) available at the bib tent after the race for €12 cash.
What about the finisher shirt? +
Marathon runners receive the shirt in their entry fee. Others could order it until 01.06. for €12. Collection from Saturday 10:00 until midnight cut-off (00:00) – including after your run. Just show your race bib. Additional stock available to purchase on the day.
Are there pacers? +
Marathon only: 3:45 and 4:15 – recognisable by large balloons.
Is there a student discount? +
Students pay 70% of the regular entry fee. Show valid student ID at bib collection.
What is the Company Champion / corporate run? +
Companies compete over 5 km at 19:15 on 20 June in auto-formed teams of 3. Combined times decide. Important: enter exactly the same company name under Club/Company at registration.
Are there time limits? +
No time limits. Official cut-off: 00:00 (midnight).
When are results and certificates available? +
Results are online after the cut-off. Certificates can be downloaded directly.
When are the award ceremonies? +
Top 3 per distance (all distances including 3 km). Ceremonies at 20:00 / 21:00 / 23:00.
Are Nordic walkers welcome? +
Yes – please start at the back of the field.
Who provides medical support? +
DRK Wiesbaden (German Red Cross) is present at start/finish and along the course. Emergency: 112.
